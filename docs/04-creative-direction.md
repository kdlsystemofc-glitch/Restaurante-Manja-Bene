## 1. VISUAL DIVERGENCE AUDIT: WHY PREVIOUS DIRECTION WAS REJECTED

The initial visual direction contained elements that risked feeling like generic restaurant templates:
* **Category Clichés Avoided:** Dark backgrounds with orange neon accents, generic SaaS Bento grid layouts, floating burger/food cutouts, and sterile white card layouts reminiscent of generic iFood delivery apps.
* **Client-First Redesign Basis:**
  1. *What makes this business visually unique?* Its 19-year heritage in Bairro Assunção, female leadership, authentic clay-pot (*caldeirão de barro*) cooking, and affordable R$ 20-40 home-cooked lunch tradition.
  2. *Design Language Materiality:* Derived directly from hand-fired clay caldeirões (Terracotta `#8D3921`), sautéed couve (Basil Green `#1E5336`), and sunlit dining tables (Warm Parchment `#FFFDF2`).
  3. *Unique Recognition:* If logo and text were removed, the clay-pot Feijoada, terracotta stroke borders, slab-serif typography (*Bitter*), and warm parchment background remain 100% unique to Restaurante Manja Bene.

## 2. THREE RADICALLY DIFFERENT VISUAL DIRECTIONS

---

### DIRECTION A: "A CADERNETA DA COZINHA" (Editorial Gazette & Kitchen Journal)
* **Concept:** Styled like a classic Brazilian kitchen gazette or hand-curated editorial journal.
* **Visual Anchor:** Newsprint cream (`#FAF6EE`), hand-drawn stroke dividers, slab serif headlines, multi-column editorial text, and pull-quotes styled like newspaper clippings.
* **Hero Composition:** Left: Large hand-set serif title *"O Almoço que Alimenta São Bernardo Desde 2007"*, stamped with a circular "Gestão de Empreendedoras" seal; Right: Full-height portrait frame of the clay caldeirão Feijoada (`imgi_16.webp`).
* **Tone:** Retro-editorial, nostalgic, traditional.

---

### DIRECTION B: "ALMOÇO DE RAÍZ" (Tactile Ceramic & Brazilian Tabletop) — RECOMMENDED
* **Concept:** Rooted in the tactile materials of a traditional Brazilian dining room — hand-fired clay caldeirões, crisp white ceramic plates, fresh green herbs, and warm sunlit tables.
* **Visual Anchor:** Terracotta Clay (`#8D3921`), Couve Green (`#1E5336`), Warm Parchment (`#FFFDF2`), and slab-serif display typography (*Bitter*) combined with modern UI fonts (*Plus Jakarta Sans*).
* **Hero Composition:** Asymmetric layered card layout — Left: Oversized display title *"Dê um olé na sua fome com o verdadeiro tempero caseiro de São Bernardo"* with 4.6★ rating badge and primary terracotta WhatsApp CTA; Right: Overlapping image frame featuring `imgi_16.webp` (Feijoada completa in clay pot) with terracotta stroke border and floating badge "🏆 Campeão do ABC".
* **Tone:** Warm, appetizing, authentic, high-contrast, deeply trustworthy.

---

### DIRECTION C: "A FEIRA & O SALÃO" (Vibrant Modern Marketplace Bistro)
* **Concept:** Inspired by vibrant Brazilian marketplace chalkboards and neighborhood bistro signage.
* **Visual Anchor:** High-contrast color blocks (Terracotta + Sun Gold + Herb Green), large expressive typography, and dynamic tabbed lunch boards.
* **Hero Composition:** Top: High-impact ticker tape *"19 Anos no Bairro Assunção • 4.6★ no Google"*; Center: 3-panel visual split featuring physical store totem (`imgi_44.jpg`), Feijoada (`imgi_16.webp`), and Parmegiana (`imgi_15.webp`); Bottom: Direct WhatsApp action strip.
* **Tone:** Energetic, vibrant, fast-paced.

---

## 3. SELECTION & RECOMMENDATION

### Selected Direction: **DIRECTION B — "ALMOÇO DE RAÍZ"**

#### Key Reasons for Selection:
1. **Unmistakable Authenticity:** Eliminates all generic restaurant clichés. Replaces cold dark mode and generic Bento cards with warm clay terracotta, couve green, and parchment tones.
2. **Material Alignment:** Directly incorporates the client's real assets (Feijoada in clay pot `imgi_16.webp`, physical store totem `imgi_44.jpg`, and home-cooked dishes).
3. **High Conversion & Usability:** High-contrast typography (*Bitter* + *Plus Jakarta Sans*) ensures maximum readability on mobile devices while addressing the peak-hour delivery objection via prominent pre-order microcopy.
