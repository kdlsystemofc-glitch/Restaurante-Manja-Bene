# 04 — CREATIVE DIRECTION: RESTAURANTE MANJA BENE (BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP)

**Client:** Restaurante Manja Bene  
**Document Version:** 2.0 (Redesign)  
**Visual Thesis:** **BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP**  

---

## 1. REJECTION OF PREVIOUS CONSERVATIVE DIRECTION

The previous direction was rejected for being too quiet and templated (cream background, serif headings, contained cards, restrained photography, visual symmetry).

---

## 2. THE NEW CREATIVE DIRECTION: BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP

* **Tone:** Warm, popular, energetic, generous, approachable, colorful, human, food-driven.
* **Layout:** Asymmetric, bold, art-directed layouts with oversized typography (`Bricolage Grotesque` 900), graphic stickers, angled badges (`-3deg`), and marquee ribbons.
* **Hero Redesign:**
  * **Massive Display Headline:** *"DÊ UM OLÉ NA SUA FOME!"* in 900-weight display type.
  * **Boundary-Breaking Photography:** Oversized Feijoada Completa (`imgi_16.webp`) and Parmegiana (`imgi_15.webp`) breaking container boundaries with thick graphite borders and rotated price tags.
  * **Operational Ticker Marquee:** Continuous scrolling text ribbon (`#C8232B` background).

---

## 3. ACCEPTANCE TEST: LOGO REMOVAL TEST

If the Manja Bene logo and name are removed, the page **CANNOT** be mistaken for any generic restaurant:
* The combination of Cantina Red (`#C8232B`), Bricolage Grotesque typography, rotated "Selo de Olé" starburst stamps, and marmita price labels is **100% unique to Restaurante Manja Bene**.
