## 1. CINEMATIC MOTION PHILOSOPHY & OBJECTIVES

Motion in *Restaurante Manja Bene* serves three specific functional purposes:
1. **Appetite & Sensation:** Enhancing the visual warmth and richness of real food photography (`imgi_16.webp`, `imgi_15.webp`) as elements scroll smoothly into view.
2. **Frictionless Navigation:** Guiding the customer seamlessly from hunger exploration to direct WhatsApp pre-ordering (*"Antecipe até 11h30"*).
3. **Local Heritage Framing:** Adding quiet, refined craftsmanship (clay pot texture elevation, badge reveals) that reflects 19 years of authentic cooking.

---

## 2. CHOREOGRAPHED SCENE ANIMATIONS

### Scene 1: Top Announcement Bar & Header Entrance
* **Animation:** Top notice bar (`#announcement-bar`) slides down softly (`150ms`).
* **Header Blur:** Sticky header applies a translucent glass effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)`) with subtle border highlight on scroll.

### Scene 2: Hero "Almoço de Raíz" Entrance
* **Staggered Text Reveal:** Eyebrow badge, H1 slogan headline, supporting text, and primary CTAs fade up with `translateY(16px)` staggered by `60ms` increments.
* **Hero Image Frame Elevation:** The Feijoada completa image container (`imgi_16.webp`) gently scales from `0.97` to `1.0` with warm clay shadow elevation (`--shadow-lg`).

### Scene 3: Dish Card Hover & Micro-interactions
* **Tactile Lift:** Dish cards in `#flagship-dishes` and `#daily-menu` lift `translateY(-4px)` on hover with clay stroke border transition (`#8D3921`).
* **Primary Button Glow:** Terracotta crimson buttons (`.btn-primary`) lift `2px` on hover with a warm shadow glow `0 8px 24px rgba(141, 57, 33, 0.35)`.
* **Dish Image Zoom:** Subtle scale `scale(1.04)` on dish photography inside rounded ceramic frames (`350ms --ease-standard`).

### Scene 4: Mobile Sticky Conversion Bar
* **Scroll-Triggered Reveal:** Appears smoothly from bottom edge (`translateY(0)`) once the user scrolls past the Hero section, ensuring instant WhatsApp ordering on mobile.

### Scene 5: Interactive Pre-Order WhatsApp Modal
* **Modal Overlay:** Smooth `250ms` backdrop fade-in (`rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
* **Modal Box Entrance:** Soft upward spring reveal (`translateY(0)` from `20px`) containing the pre-order form.

---

## 3. ACCESSIBILITY & REDUCED MOTION

* **Fallback Strategy:** When `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` is enabled, all motion transforms and scale transitions are disabled and replaced with instant or simple opacity fades (`150ms`).
