# 02 — DESIGN SYSTEM: RESTAURANTE MANJA BENE (BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP)

**Client:** Restaurante Manja Bene  
**Document Version:** 2.0 (Redesign)  
**Visual Thesis:** **BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP**  

---

## 1. COLOR SYSTEM

Derived from real establishment signage (`imgi_44.jpg`), fresh ingredients, and popular Brazilian cantina culture:

* **Manja Vermelho Cantina (`#C8232B` / `#9B1B22`):** Primary bold pop color for headers, badges, and CTA highlights.
* **Amarelo Queijo & Farofa (`#F59E0B` / `#FEF3C7`):** Accent gold for stickers, starburst stamps, and pricing tags.
* **Couve & Tempero Green (`#15803D` / `#DCFCE7`):** Fresh herb green accent for operational notices and delivery badges.
* **Papel de Mesa Cream (`#FFFBEB` / `#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast warm paper canvas.
* **Preto Grafite Ink (`#18181B`):** Deep charcoal ink text (AAA 18:1 contrast) and bold graphical borders (`3px solid #18181B`).

---

## 2. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM

* **Display / Headlines:** `Bricolage Grotesque` (Google Fonts, Weights: 800, 900) — Bold, expressive display font with massive personality, tight tracking (`-0.03em`), and dramatic scale.
* **Subheaders & UI Buttons:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 700, 800).
* **Body Text:** `Inter` (Weights: 400, 600).

---

## 3. SIGNATURE DESIGN DEVICES FOR MANJA BENE

1. **"Selo de Olé" Starburst Stamp:** Rotated circular/starburst badge (*"19 ANOS • DÊ UM OLÉ NA FOME"*) angled at `-4deg` or `+6deg`.
2. **"Etiqueta de Marmita" Price Tags:** Yellow price tags (`R$ 20-40`) with thick graphite borders (`3px solid #18181B`) angled over dish cards.
3. **"Fita da Cantina" Running Marquee Ticker:** High-contrast moving ticker ribbon (`#C8232B` background with white scrolling text) separating major scenes.
