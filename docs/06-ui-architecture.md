# 06 — UI ARCHITECTURE: RESTAURANTE MANJA BENE (BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP)

**Client:** Restaurante Manja Bene  
**Document Version:** 2.0 (Redesign)  
**Visual Thesis:** **BRAZILIAN CANTINA POP**  

---

## PAGE ARCHITECTURE & SECTION SEQUENCE

1. **Section 01: Top Running Marquee Ribbon (`#marquee-ribbon`)**
   * High-contrast moving ticker ribbon (`#C8232B` background with white bold text).
2. **Section 02: Global Cantina Header (`#main-header`)**
   * Pop logo badge with "Desde 2007" tag, navigation links, and primary WhatsApp button with thick graphite border.
3. **Section 03: HERO POP (`#hero`)**
   * Giant H1 headline *"DÊ UM OLÉ NA SUA FOME"*, rotated "Selo de Olé" starburst badge (`-4deg`), oversized Feijoada image frame (`imgi_16.webp`) with yellow price tag (`R$ 20-40`), and instant WhatsApp CTA button.
4. **Section 04: O BANQUETE DOS CAMPEÕES (`#flagship-dishes`)**
   * Full-width editorial showcase featuring Parmegiana Especial (`imgi_15.webp`) and Feijoada Completa (`imgi_16.webp`) with bold quotes and ingredient tags.
5. **Section 05: CARDÁPIO DA CASA (`#daily-menu`)**
   * Asymmetric dish card layout with bold category pills, thick graphite card borders (`3px solid #18181B`), and pre-order buttons.
6. **Section 06: ALMOÇO SEM FILA (`#friction-relief`)**
   * Bold 3-step cantina delivery cards with rotated headers addressing peak-hour lunch pre-orders (*"Antecipe até 11h30"*).
7. **Section 07: VOZ DO ASSUNÇÃO (`#social-proof`)**
   * High-impact customer testimonial cards with Google 4.6★ rating integration.
8. **Section 08: LOCALIZAÇÃO & QUADRO DO MANJA (`#location-info`)**
   * Real photo of the physical establishment totem (`imgi_44.jpg`), address, operating hours, and Google Maps route button.
9. **Section 09: FOOTER BANNER & CREDITS (`#footer`)**
   * Full-width Cantina Red block with giant slogan *"DÊ UM OLÉ NA SUA FOME!"* and copyright details.
10. **Section 10: MOBILE STICKY CONVERSION BAR & PRE-ORDER MODAL**
    * Mobile fixed bottom CTA bar and interactive WhatsApp pre-order modal.
